How to use amitfpl

Home - the at-a-glance dashboard: next fixtures and quick picks.

Players - every player, sortable and filterable. Click a player anywhere for the full profile.

Planner - build a squad, drag players between pitch and bench, pick captain and chips, and plan transfers for future gameweeks (free transfers bank up, extra moves cost -4). 🔗 Share moves your plan between devices.

Scout - captain picks per gameweek, likely scorers, differentials, and best value.

Market - price changes and transfer momentum.

Status - injuries, doubts, and suspension risk.

Compare - 2-3 players side by side.

Fixtures - difficulty planner, goal & clean sheet forecast, rotation pairs, blanks & doubles.

Lineups - our projected starting XI for every club.

Matches - every gameweek's games; scores and point events fill in live.

Set Pieces - penalty, free-kick and corner takers.

My Team - connect your real FPL squad with your Team ID.

What's new

Elo-powered model v2 - team strength from daily ClubElo ratings, defensive-contribution and card modelling, penalty-taker boost.

Season planner - per-GW transfers with free-transfer banking and -4 hits, chips, share links between devices.

Predicted lineups, scoring & assist chances, market movers and price trend charts in player profiles.

Telegram alerts - deadline reminders and watchlist news, sent from the cloud.

Glossary

xP - expected points (our model's forecast).

xG / xA - expected goals / assists, quality of chances involved in.

xGI - expected goal involvements (xG + xA).

DC - defensive contribution points (tackles, blocks, interceptions, clearances).

FDR - fixture difficulty rating, 1 (easiest) to 5 (hardest).

Sel % - how many FPL managers own the player.

FT - free transfers available (bank up to 5; extra moves cost -4 points).

TC / BB / WC / FH - Triple Captain, Bench Boost, Wildcard, Free Hit chips.
